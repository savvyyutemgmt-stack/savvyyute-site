How this works

This is where you change the Savvy Yute website. Nothing goes live until someone clicks Deploy to live site.

Making a change

  1. Open Edit website. Every section of the site is listed on the left: Theme settings, Header and menu, Hero banner, Services, Portfolio, Ventures, Partners, Contact and footer, SEO and sharing.
  2. Click a section to open it. The live preview on the right jumps to that part of the site. Click another section any time; you never leave the page.
  3. Change the text, colours or photos, then click Save at the top.
  4. While you edit, the live preview on the right shows the real website with your changes as you type. Use the eye icon at the top of the editor to show or hide it.
  5. Happy with it? Set the status to Ready, then click Publish and choose Publish now. This moves it to staging (your dev version). It is not live yet and costs nothing.
  6. When you're ready for the public to see it, click Deploy to live site in the bottom-left corner. Everything published since the last deploy goes live together. This is the only step that uses a Netlify deploy.

What "Workflow" means

Workflow is the board of changes that aren't live yet. Each saved change is a card that moves left to right:

DraftsBeing worked on
In reviewCheck the preview link
ReadyApproved
PublishSaved to staging (dev)

Any admin can see everyone's drafts here. Delete a draft card if you change your mind. After publishing, the bottom-left bar shows Published changes waiting to go live until someone clicks Deploy to live site.

Photos

  • Any field marked with an image button lets you upload from your computer or pick a photo you've uploaded before.
  • All uploads live under Media at the top. You can upload or delete photos there too.
  • Keep photos under 1 MB so the site stays fast. JPG for photos, PNG for logos.
  • Sizes that work: headline background 1600 px wide or more; client cards portrait, about 900 x 1200; share image 1200 x 630.

Tips

  • Drag items in a list to reorder them.
  • Each section has a Show this section switch. Turning it off hides it. Nothing is deleted.
  • Colour fields: click the swatch to pick, or paste a code like #ff3b1f.
