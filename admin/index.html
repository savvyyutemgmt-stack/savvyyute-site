How this works

This is where you change the Savvy Yute website. Nothing you do here goes live until you publish it.

Making a change

  1. Click Contents at the top, then pick a section on the left (1 to 8).
  2. Open the item, change the text, colours or photos, then click Save at the top.
  3. While you edit, the live preview on the right shows the real website with your changes as you type. Use the eye icon at the top of the editor to show or hide it.
  4. After saving, your change is a draft. Click Check for Preview (or View preview) to open it on a test version of the site you can share.
  5. Happy with it? Set the status to Ready, then click Publish and choose Publish now. The live site updates in about a minute.

What "Workflow" means

Workflow is the board of changes that aren't live yet. Each saved change is a card that moves left to right:

DraftsBeing worked on
In reviewCheck the preview link
ReadyApproved
PublishLive on the website

Any admin can see everyone's drafts here. Delete a draft card if you change your mind; the live site stays as it was.

Photos

  • Any field marked with an image button lets you upload from your computer or pick a photo you've uploaded before.
  • All uploads live under Media at the top. You can upload or delete photos there too.
  • Keep photos under 1 MB so the site stays fast. JPG for photos, PNG for logos.
  • Sizes that work: headline background 1600 px wide or more; client cards portrait, about 900 x 1200; share image 1200 x 630.

Tips

  • Drag items in a list to reorder them.
  • Switching a section off in 2 hides it. Nothing is deleted.
  • Colour fields: click the swatch to pick, or paste a code like #ff3b1f.
